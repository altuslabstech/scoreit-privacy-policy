Privacy Policy

Score It!

Last updated: October 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Score It! ("Score It!" or "the app"), developed by Altus Labs, handles your information.

Your scores, under your control. Score It! stores matches on your device and its scoring features work offline. Android Settings includes advertising provided by Google AdMob. There are no accounts or developer-operated match servers. You choose whether to share a result. Your operating system may back up or transfer app data.

1Information the app handles

Score It! does not require registration, sign-in, an email address, or a phone number. Altus Labs does not operate a server that collects your match records. The app includes Google AdMob, which may automatically collect information for advertising as described below.

The app processes information you enter or generate while keeping score, including:

  • Player or team names and their selected colors.
  • Game choices, match rules, scores, rounds, scoring events, and sport statistics.
  • Match dates, progress, results, and saved history.
  • App preferences, such as your theme choice.

This information is used locally to operate the scorekeeper, save and resume matches, support undo, display history, and create results you choose to export.

2Local data storage

Matches are stored in a SQLite database in the app's private storage. Preferences are stored locally on your device. Altus Labs does not operate a backend that receives or stores these records, and the app does not provide an online account or cloud-sync service.

Operating-system backups and transfers may create copies outside the app's local storage, as explained below.

3Advertising, analytics, and device access

Score It! uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob) to display native advertisements in Android Settings. The development and testing configuration uses Google's sample ad identifiers. Advertising requires an internet connection; core scoring features work offline.

Google may automatically collect and share the following information through its advertising SDK:

  • Approximate location, which may be estimated from the device's IP address.
  • App interactions, including interactions with advertisements.
  • Diagnostic information, such as SDK performance and error information.
  • Device or other identifiers, such as the Advertising ID and App Set ID, where applicable.

Google may use this information to provide and measure advertising, perform analytics, prevent fraud, and support security and compliance. The app currently requests non-personalized ads; these requests may still involve identifiers and other information for purposes such as fraud prevention and measurement. Altus Labs does not maintain its own database of this advertising information.

Read the Google Privacy Policy and Google's advertising information for details about Google's processing and available controls.

The app does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, or precise location. Results are created in the app's temporary storage and shared through the operating system's share sheet; sharing does not require access to your entire photo or file library.

4Sharing results and third-party services

If you choose to share a result, Score It! creates PNG images or a PDF containing the selected match's information. Depending on the game, this can include player or team names, scores, dates, rounds, and statistics.

The app passes these files to the app or destination you select through the system share sheet. Review the result before sharing. Receiving apps and services handle the files according to their own privacy policies, and may upload or retain them.

Altus Labs does not sell your information or send your match records to AdMob. Google may process and share advertising-related information through its SDK as described above and under its own policies. Copies you share are outside Score It!'s control.

5Operating-system backups and transfers

Your operating system may back up or transfer match data and preferences according to your device settings, platform version, and backup provider. This may include cloud backup or migration to another device. Altus Labs does not receive or manage these backups.

On Android 9–11, the app's cloud-backup rules require client-side encryption. On Android 12 and later, cloud-backup rules require encryption capabilities, and separate rules allow device-to-device transfer. Older Android versions use the operating system's standard backup protections.

Use your device and backup-provider settings to manage backups and any retained copies. Restoring a backup may restore previously saved app data.

6Data security

Local records are protected by the operating system's application sandbox and your device's security controls. Score It! does not add separate database encryption or an in-app lock. Protect your device with a screen lock and keep its software up to date.

No storage method can guarantee absolute security. Exported files are readable by the recipients you choose and by anyone to whom those recipients provide access.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK uses encrypted connections to protect advertising data in transit.

7Data retention and deletion

Saved matches remain locally until you delete them or remove the app's data. You can:

  • Delete individual saved matches from their match details.
  • Use Settings → Delete all data to delete all match records, including matches in progress. This action does not reset your theme preference.
  • Clear the app's storage through Android settings or uninstall the app to remove its local data and preferences.

Temporary export files may remain in the app's cache so receiving apps can open them. When a new export is created, the app attempts to remove exports older than 24 hours; this is not a guaranteed automatic deletion deadline. The operating system may also clear cached files.

Deleting matches does not delete files already shared, copies saved elsewhere, or existing operating-system backups. Manage those copies with their respective apps or backup provider. Altus Labs cannot retrieve or delete local matches remotely.

Information processed by Google AdMob is retained and deleted according to Google's policies. Android and Google privacy settings provide controls for advertising identifiers and advertising preferences.

8Children's privacy

Score It! does not require accounts, and Altus Labs does not knowingly collect children's personal information through its own servers. Names entered for scoring are stored locally like other match data. Parents and guardians can manage local records and sharing using the controls described in this policy. Information processed by Google AdMob is subject to Google's policies and applicable requirements for that service.

9Information you send to support

If you contact Altus Labs by email, we receive your email address and the message or attachments you choose to send. We use this information to respond to your inquiry and address support issues, and retain it only as needed for those purposes or applicable legal obligations. Email is processed by our email provider under its own privacy practices.

You may contact us to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to any applicable retention obligations. Please avoid sending sensitive information or match records unless necessary for your request.

10Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app's features, data practices, or applicable requirements change. Updates will appear on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. This policy describes the current version of Score It!.
